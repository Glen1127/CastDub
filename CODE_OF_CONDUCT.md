# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and focus discussion on the
project and its users. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing,
and publication of private media or voice data are not acceptable.

## Project-specific responsibilities

Contributors must not submit media, subtitles, voices, credentials, or model
weights they lack permission to distribute. Do not identify voice actors or
infer sensitive traits from submitted recordings. Report accidental exposure
privately as described in `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, unsafe, or
inconsistent with this standard. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate
and protect affected people and private data.

## Reporting

Use the repository's private vulnerability reporting channel for confidential
reports. For other conduct concerns, contact the project maintainers through a
private channel rather than opening an issue containing personal information.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
