- WhisperX — BSD-2-Clause; ASR and forced-alignment provider.
- pyannote.audio — MIT; speaker diarisation code. Individual model terms are tracked separately.
- pyannote speaker-diarization-community-1 — CC-BY-4.0 and gated Hugging Face access conditions.
- MLX-Audio — MIT; Apple Silicon audio inference runtime.
- Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base — Apache-2.0; primary cross-language voice-cloning model.
- CosyVoice — Apache-2.0 code; optional provider whose selected model card must be reviewed before use.

Exact versions, model revisions, checksums, and accepted terms must be written to each job's provenance report.
